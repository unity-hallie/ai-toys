Mathematical Concepts

Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

Dimensionality reduction technique used to compress 384D embeddings to 24D.

Citation: Jolliffe, I. T. (2002). Principal Component Analysis (2nd ed.). Springer-Verlag.

Mathematics
Dimensionality Reduction
Leech Lattice

24-dimensional geometric structure with exceptional symmetry properties. Chosen as the target dimension for PCA compression due to its mathematical properties and potential for emergent structure in semantic projection.

References:

  • Conway, J. H., & Sloane, N. J. A. (1999). Sphere Packings, Lattices, and Groups (3rd ed.). Springer.
  • Viazovska, M. (2016). The Sphere Packing Problem in Dimension 8. Annals of Mathematics, 185(3), 991-1015.

Architecture & Methodology

Sequence Prediction in Latent Space

The Predictor model learns to predict chunk_{i+1} from chunk_i in the 24D latent space, capturing directional semantic tendency specific to each text/persona.

Related work:

  • Bengio, Y., Simard, P., & Frasconi, P. (1994). Learning Long-Term Dependencies with Gradient Descent is Difficult. IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks, 5(2), 157-166.
  • Hochreiter, S., Bengio, Y., Frasconi, P., & Schmidhuber, J. (2001). Gradient Flow in Recurrent Nets: The Difficulty of Learning Long-Term Dependencies. In A Field Guide to Dynamical Recurrent Networks.
Deep Learning
Sequence Models
Personalized Language Models

The approach of training separate lightweight models per persona, rather than a single large model, relates to work in style transfer and author modeling.

Related work:
