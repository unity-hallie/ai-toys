Magic 8 Ball Interactive Analysis

Explore patterns across 12 personas, 8 questions, 96 readings.

Interactive Tools

Use these tools to discover relationships, patterns, and significance in the oracle data. Each tool reveals something different about how personas make decisions.

Are Two Personas Actually Different?

Pick two personas. We'll compare their decision patterns and test whether their differences are real or just noise. This teaches you how to use statistics to compare models.

Which Questions Make Personas Disagree Most?

Pick a question. See how each persona answered it. Some questions create consensus; others spark disagreement. This reveals which questions are most diagnostic.

Which Personas Have Strong Biases?

Pick a response (like "Yes, absolutely"). See which personas prefer it, how confidently, and when. This reveals model biases and personality quirks.

How Often Are Models Confident?

Drag the slider to filter by confidence. At high confidence, we only see clear decisions. At low confidence, we see ambiguous cases. What does this tell us about model certainty?

Statistical Significance

Compare two personas statistically. Shows t-statistic, p-value, and Cohen's d (effect size). p < 0.05 = significantly different.

Methodology: Comparisons use euclidean distance and t-tests. Significance threshold: p < 0.05. Effect size (Cohen's d): <0.2 = small, 0.2–0.8 = medium, >0.8 = large.
