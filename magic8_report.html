What This Reveals
High confidence readings show when personas are most certain. Low confidence readings show when the latent space is ambiguous between multiple responses. This gap indicates decision clarity variance across personas and questions.
Methodology: Each persona was trained on a distinct literary text using PCA (384D → 24D) and a predictor network (24D → 24D). Responses are matched via cosine similarity. Confidence is the similarity score to the closest matching response option.
Data Quality: No filtering. All 96 readings reported as generated.
